Educational purpose & accuracy disclaimer

Hominin History is an educational and informational project about human evolution, paleoanthropology, and archaeogenetics. It is written for a general audience by an independent enthusiast, not a credentialed academic institution. We work hard to base every article on museum sources and peer-reviewed literature and to flag genuine scientific debate where it exists.

That said, human evolution is a fast-moving field, and content may become outdated as new fossils and genetic studies appear. Nothing here should be treated as professional, academic, medical, or genetic-counseling advice. Interpretations of ancestry, reconstructions, and evolutionary relationships reflect current mainstream science as we understand it, and reasonable experts sometimes disagree. Always consult primary sources and qualified professionals for authoritative or personal guidance. If you spot an error, please tell us — we correct mistakes.
