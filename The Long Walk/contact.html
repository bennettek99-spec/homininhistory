Questions, corrections, or a fossil you think we should cover — the inbox is open.

The Long Walk is a one-person, independent project, and hearing from readers is one of the best parts of running it. Whether you've found a factual slip, want a source we cited, have an idea for an article, or just want to say the timeline helped with a school project — please reach out.

Email bennettkuhn@homininhistory.com

What to get in touch about

  • Corrections. Human evolution moves fast and mistakes happen. Point us to a source and we'll fix errors and note the update.
  • Questions. Curious how we clustered the ancient-DNA samples, or why a species sits where it does on the tree? Ask away.
  • Article & source suggestions. Want a comparison or topic covered? Send it over — reader requests shape the content calendar.
  • Educational & permissions. Teachers and students are welcome to use the site; for anything beyond short attributed quotes, ask first.
  • Press & collaboration. Happy to talk about the project and how it's built.

Response time

This is a passion project run around school and life, so replies usually take a few days — sometimes a little longer during busy stretches. Every genuine message gets read, and correction requests are prioritized.
