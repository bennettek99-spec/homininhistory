Image credits & licences

Every species image on this site comes from Wikimedia Commons under a free licence. Here is who made each one, and the terms it is used under.

Hominin History uses freely licensed images so that the whole site can be read, shared and reused without ambiguity. Each image below links back to its original page on Wikimedia Commons, where the full licence text and the uploader's own description are available.

Where an image is a facial reconstruction, it is an interpretive scientific artwork, not a photograph of a living individual. Skeletal proportions are constrained by the fossil evidence; skin tone, hair, eye colour and soft tissue are informed inference. Where an image is a fossil or cast, it shows real skeletal material, though many museum specimens on display are casts rather than the original bone.

All images have been cropped and resized to fit this site's layout. No other alterations have been made. Images under a ShareAlike licence remain under that licence in their cropped form, and may be reused on the same terms.
